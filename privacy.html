Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Applies to ElHalawany Tube for TV (com.elhalawany.tube.tv) and ElHalawany Tube for Car (com.elhalawany.tube.car).

1. Who we are

ElHalawany Tube is developed by Basem M. ElHalawany. Contact: basem.mamdoh@feng.bu.edu.eg.

2. YouTube API Services

ElHalawany Tube uses YouTube API Services to show your subscriptions, playlists, search results and videos, and plays videos with YouTube's official embedded player. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What the app accesses and why

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, the app requests only these permissions:

PermissionWhat it is used for
Basic profile: name, email, profile picture (openid, email, profile)To show which family member is signed in on the device.
Read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly)To list the channels you subscribe to, your playlists, and to search videos. The app cannot upload, comment, like, subscribe, unsubscribe or change anything on your account.
Files created by the app in your Google Drive (drive.file)To create and update one small file named "ElHalawany Tube - continue watching.json" with the last video you watched and where you stopped, so you can continue on your other device (TV ↔ car). With this permission the app can only access files it created itself; it cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

4. What is stored, and where

  • On your device only: sign-in tokens (encrypted with Android Keystore), your profiles, favorites, watch history and resume positions, kids-profile settings (allowed channels, daily limit, hours, watch-time log), venue-mode settings, and the app language. This data is excluded from device backups.
  • In your own Google Drive (only if you signed in): the "continue watching" file described above. You can see it in your Drive and delete it at any time.
  • Anonymous usage counter: once a day the app sends our small server (a Google Apps Script) a random install number created on the device, the device type (TV or car), the app version, and how many times each feature was used (for example kids profiles, venue mode, phone link). It contains no name, email, Google account, channel, video or watch history, and is used only to count users and see which features are useful.
  • Venue screens (only if you use venue mode): the place's display settings (place type, name, scrolling message, playlist links and on/off hours) are kept on our server under a random venue code, so the owner can manage all the place's screens from the phone. The owner's control key is stored only as a one-way hash. Leaving venue mode stops the TV from using it.
  • Nothing else. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Data shared with Google and YouTube

The app talks directly to Google and YouTube services (sign-in, YouTube Data API, YouTube's public channel feeds, the YouTube embedded player, and Google Drive). YouTube may show ads and collect data in its player under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Microphone (car app)

The car app can use the microphone only when you press the microphone button, to understand voice commands such as "play" or "next". Speech is handled by your device's speech recognition service; ElHalawany Tube does not record, store or send your voice anywhere.

7. Phone link (TV app)

When you choose "From phone", the TV opens a temporary page on your home network, protected by a one-time code in the QR code, so your phone can send a YouTube link, a video link, or a video file to play on the TV. A file sent this way is kept only while it plays and is deleted afterwards. The page closes when the window closes and is not reachable from the internet.

7b. Messages from the developer

The app may show a short announcement from the developer (for example news about a new version). It is read from the same public settings file used for updates and is shown once; nothing is sent back.

8. Children

Kids profiles are created and controlled by a parent on the device, with a PIN. Their settings and watch-time log stay on the device and are only shown to the parent.

9. Removing your data

  • Sign out or delete a profile in the app: its tokens and data are deleted from the device.
  • Uninstall the app: all data on the device is removed.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/connections (or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). You can also delete the file "ElHalawany Tube - continue watching.json" from your Google Drive.

10. Google API Services User Data Policy

ElHalawany Tube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
